## Overview
Led development of an enterprise conversational AI assistant serving support engineers across HPE. The system analyzed and retrieved answers from a repository of 3470 process documents, reducing response time for support queries and cutting repeat escalations by ~40%. Evolved from rule-based retrieval to LLM-assisted, retrieval-augmented architecture during the project lifecycle.
